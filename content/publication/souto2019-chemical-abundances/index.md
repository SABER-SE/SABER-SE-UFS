abstract: |
  We present a detailed characterization of the M-dwarf Ross 128 (GJ 447) and its recently discovered temperate Earth-like planet Ross 128b, using high-resolution near-infrared APOGEE spectra and stellar evolutionary models. We derive fundamental stellar parameters (T_eff = 3,231 K, log g = 4.97, [Fe/H] = -0.02) and chemical abundances for 11 elements. Using these results as input to planetary interior models, we show that Ross 128b is consistent with an Earth-like rocky composition if it falls within a narrow range of planet masses. Our analysis demonstrates the power of combining high-resolution spectroscopy with planetary interior modeling to characterize potentially habitable exoplanets.

Neste trabalho, combinamos espectroscopia de alta resolução com modelos de evolução estelar e de interior planetário para caracterizar o sistema Ross 128. O planeta Ross 128b é um dos candidatos a planeta temperado mais próximos da Terra e este trabalho fornece um dos primeiros estudos detalhados de sua composição provável.
